A port being open tells you that something is there. A version tells you what it is and roughly how old. Versions are the raw material of the judgement phase, because a known issue is attached to a version, not to a port. In this lesson you will ask a service to identify itself, first with a scan and then by reading the banner it sends. You will also see why the same version string can matter a great deal or not at all, depending on where it is.

## What you'll be able to do

- Identify a service and its version with `nmap -sV`.
- Read a service banner directly with `curl -I`.
- Say why a version number matters more than the port it sits on.
- Tell what `-sV` can and cannot work out.

## Step 1: Start something to identify

A scan can only identify a service that is running, so start one on purpose. Open a second Termux session by swiping in from the left edge of the screen and tapping New session. In that session, start a throwaway web server on a high port.
